Champion Combo Build

Single-target damage for the Emperium Festival: the Monk combo with Berserk Knuckle, bleed, poison and burn. Built from the 0.2.7 game code for a 40-minute Hell 15 Oath 20 run. Still being tested: this page changes as the in-game results come in.

How it works

1Swing very fastIncrease AGI and Beelzebub push you to ~276 hits per swing.
2Combo every 1.17 sTrifecta, Quadruple Blow, Raging Thrust. Every combo hit is multiplied by hits per swing0.7 (×51).
3It all counts as basic attackSo the combo crits, bleeds, poisons, burns and keeps the freeze mark.

The 3 golden rules

  1. Raging Thrust is your best skill. It over-levels with no limit: ×72 at Lv 70.
  2. No attack skills. Banish Asura Strike and Chain Crush Combo. Chain Crush spends your 30 combo charges (×2.2).
  3. VIT 15 is enough. It already reaches 50 effective VIT, the cap for both VIT defenses. Every other point goes to damage.

Hell 15 Oath 20 run

At Hell 15 every monster hits harder than the cap, so each plain hit is the cap: 230% of your max HP at Oath 20. Then the cuts below apply, in this order (player.gd take_damage).

VIT 50Hit cap −50% and damage −25%
Steel Body 5−75% (the cap), up all the time with your haste
Armor 57−66%
Cape grade S−15%
Hel's Pact+20% damage taken per boon

One plain hit takes

0 boons: 6% of max HP · 5 boons: 13% · 11 boons: 20%
Max HP does not change this: plain hits are a share of max HP.

Not capped

Hazards and MVP mechanics ignore the hit cap. Those are what end runs: dodge them.

Which god altars to pray at

Valkyrie+20% damage, +10% max HP. Its bad side is a stronger MVP.
Hel+10% damage that multiplies. The −15% max HP doesn't matter here: plain hits are a share of max HP anyway.
Freyja+8% crit, +15% crit damage. Monsters +20% damage, but they already hit past the cap.
Yggdrasil+1 extra life and +2 HP/s. No downside.
Niflheim+35% EXP means more levels, but monsters get +25% HP.
Thor, Odin, LokiSkill damage, procs and cards: nothing for this build.

Checked in the code

  • Combo timing (main.gd monk_combo): 0.09 s per hit, 0.25 s between parts, then the next swing: one combo every 1.17 s.
  • Combo damage: basic attack × part (Quadruple 3.2 + 0.7/level, Thrust 8 + 2/level, over-levelling) × (1 + 0.04 per charge, max 30) × hits per swing0.7. Raging Thrust also hits the target through its line at 60%.
  • Critical Explosion: always up (15 s duration, 16 s cooldown cut by haste). On a crit, 35% of hits chain a ki hit at 50% to the same Emperium; it gets no Berserk fury.
  • Steamed Tongue is reset after every swing, so it boosts basic swings only, not combo hits. It is still the best dish.
  • Grace reaches Blessing, Increase AGI, Signum and Steel Body (Acolyte and Monk skills), not Critical Explosion's crit.
  • Level-ups: one pick per level; its rarity gives 1–3 levels and LUK shifts the odds. The tables assume 3 points per level, as seen in a real Nv 530 save.

  • Critical Explosion: siempre activo (15 s de duración, 16 s de recarga reducida por el haste). En crítico, el 35% de los golpes encadena un golpe de ki al 50% al mismo Emperium; no recibe la furia de la Berserk.
  • Steamed Tongue se reinicia tras cada ataque, así que solo mejora los ataques básicos, no los golpes del combo. Aun así es el mejor plato.
  • Grace afecta a Blessing, Increase AGI, Signum y Steel Body (habilidades de Acolyte y Monk), no al crítico de Critical Explosion.
  • Subidas de nivel: una elección por nivel; su rareza da 1–3 niveles y la LUK cambia las probabilidades. Las tablas suponen 3 puntos por nivel, como en una build guardada real de Nv 530.

祭典之前

重要：保存的配置會保留等級、技能、能力值、裝備、神壇與 Hel 印記。契約、寵物與料理則是進入祭典時身上的。

祭典契約

Agony (苦痛契約) · Grace (恩典契約) · Affinity (親和契約)

祭典料理

Steamed Tongue · Desert Scorpions · Mastela Wine
  • 寵物: Knight of Windstorm
  • 設定中開啟 Death Sentence（對頭目 +30%；Emperium 算頭目）
  • 在遊戲結束畫面保存配置（「Guardar build」）。

程式碼驗證

  • 連段時間（main.gd monk_combo）：每下 0.09 秒、段間 0.25 秒、接著下一次揮擊：每 1.17 秒一套。
  • 連段傷害：普攻 × 段數倍率（四連擊 3.2 + 0.7/級、猛龍誇強 8 + 2/級，可超等級）×（1 + 每層充能 0.04，最多 30）× 每次揮擊命中數0.7。猛龍誇強的直線也會以 60% 再打一次目標。
  • Critical Explosion：全程開啟（持續 15 秒，冷卻 16 秒再被縮減）。爆擊時 35% 機率對同一個 Emperium 追加 50% 氣功，沒有狂怒加成。
  • Steamed Tongue 每次揮擊後重置，只加普攻、不加連段，但仍是最好的料理。
  • 恩典契約作用於 Blessing、Increase AGI、Signum 與 Steel Body（服事與武僧技能），不作用於 Critical Explosion 的爆擊。
  • 升級：每級一次選擇，稀有度給 1–3 級，LUK 會改變機率。表格以每級 3 點計算，來自真實的 530 級存檔。
