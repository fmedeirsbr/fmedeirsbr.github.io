Kagerou Bleed Build

The build behind a rank-1 Emperium Festival run: basic-attack bleeds, two daggers, shadows. For players who already own Legendary gear and MVP cards.

How it works

1Hit very fastYour basic attack hits many times every second, and your shadows copy every hit.
2Every hit cutsEach hit makes the target bleed. The bleed stores the damage.
3BurstAt 5 cuts all the stored damage explodes at once: about 96% of your damage.

The 3 golden rules

  1. Left Hand Mastery is your best skill. Almost every level-up goes into it.
  2. Only basic attacks. Banish the throwing and ninjutsu skills so the game never offers them.
  3. The run makes your festival build. The stronger you finish the run (level, Hel Marks), the more Emperiums you break.

Why these pieces work together

Two daggers. The left-hand dagger gets stronger with every Left Hand Mastery level. At high levels it counts like 8 daggers.
Kathryne Keyron and Angeling give +1 hit per swing and +1 shadow. More shadows, more hits, more bleeding.
Glacial Heart keeps freezing the Emperium. Wind daggers hit frozen targets much harder.
Argiope and Diabolus Ring poison the target. Every poison stack makes it take more damage from everything.

Where the level-ups of a level ~400 run should end up.

Put points in

AGI, DEX and LUK, about the same in each (80–90 each at level 400). VIT 50–70 if you need to survive.

Never

STR and INT. Ninja Aura already gives you plenty of both. Banish them at the start of the run.
Getting Signum Crucis. It is not a Ninja skill: you copy it with Plagiarism. Play a Thief (or Rogue / Assassin) run in Rachel Sanctuary and defeat the Beholder Master. Then pick Signum as your shared Plagiarism skill and it shows up in your Ninja's level-ups.

During the run

Pacts

Blood · Agony · Grace
Grace helps you heal and survive.

Dishes

Desert Scorpions · Mastela Wine · Immortal Stew
Stew gives max HP for every kill.

Niflheim route (dark path)

  • Open every Corrupt Chest: each Hel Mark is +8% damage forever in that build.
  • Win the Niflheim MVP raids: each win is a big damage boost, but you also take more damage.
  • Survive to 40:00: more time, more levels, more damage.

Which god altars to pray at

ValkyrieThe best one: damage that multiplies everything. Always take it.
FreyjaGood: more critical damage.
HelA little damage, but costs 15% max HP.
Odin, Thor, Niflheim, LokiSkip them: no help for bleeding, and you still get their bad side.

Before the Emperium Festival

Important: the festival uses your pacts, pet and dishes from right now, not from the run. Change them before you go.

Festival pacts

Blood · Agony · Affinity
Break Grace, take Affinity (Elements tab).

Festival dishes

Steamed Tongue · Desert Scorpions · Mastela Wine
  • Pet: Knight of Windstorm
  • Death Sentence on in settings (+30% vs bosses; the Emperium is a boss)
  • Save the pacts and dishes as a set called "Festival", so switching is one click
  • After the festival, switch back to your run set (Grace and Immortal Stew)
